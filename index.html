About

Short bio

I am a PhD candidate at the Berlin School of Economics (BSoE), Humboldt University of Berlin, supervised by Prof. Roland Strausz and Prof. Sebastian Schweighofer-Kodritsch. My research interests are auctions, information economics, market design, and mechanism design.

In September 2026, I will join Mercator School of Management, University of Duisburg-Essen as a postdoc.
