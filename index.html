Entry Coordination in Auctions with Entry Costs

Jia Wei Zhang · Working paper · Revision requested by Games and Economic Behavior.
Private entry recommendations let the seller capture all surplus net of entry costs by randomizing between the efficient entrant count and one more.
SSRN

Performance Contracts for Noncontractible Recommendations

Yushan Meng, Jia Wei Zhang · Working paper
With private advice costs, at most two performance contracts attain the seller’s best delegated payoff under favorable equilibrium selection; direct commitment weakly improves that payoff.
SSRN

Evidence Rules and the Value of Silence

Jia Wei Zhang · Working paper
Strict evidence requirements can induce more investigation yet worsen decisions because the extra evidence fails to compensate for ignoring informative silence.

About

Short bio

I am a postdoc at the Mercator School of Management, University of Duisburg-Essen.

I expect to get my PhD in 2026 at the Berlin School of Economics (BSoE), Humboldt University of Berlin, supervised by Prof. Roland Strausz and Prof. Sebastian Schweighofer-Kodritsch. My research interests are auctions, information economics, market design, and mechanism design.
